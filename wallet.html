Connect your wallet — your keys, your signature

Foreman never custodies funds. The covenant holds the budget; your wallet only signs. Pick any connector below — they all end at the same ChunkPod.

1 · WizardConnect wiz://

The full signing path, proven on Chipnet (tx c47e0d6e…).

1. Open the live demo wallet card in the full server app (node server.mjs).

2. Press Pair a wallet and scan the QR with Cake Wallet, Cashonize, Zapit or any WizardConnect wallet.

3. Press Fund this pod from my wallet. The server builds the tx, your phone signs it over the relay, the server verifies the pod output is exactly the amount requested, then broadcasts. A wallet that signs anything else gets a 400 — nothing is sent.

2 · XO Template XO.cash

The same covenant as an XO wallet template, validated by the XO engine.

Template ID: 6bc7202420bc83f5…
Roles: client + provider.
Transactions: fund · pull a chunk · refund.

Import xo/foreman.pod.template.json from the repo into XO and sign with XO's own flow.

3 · Paytaca / Selene / any BCH wallet

No special integration needed to fund a pod: create a pod in the demo's Advanced section, copy its pod address, and send BCH to it directly from Paytaca, Selene, or any standard BCH wallet. The covenant rules apply the moment the coins land in the pod.

4 · xpub — read-only

No phone handy? Paste an account xpub in the demo's wallet card and Foreman derives the same receive addresses read-only — enough to check balances and fund a pod, never enough to sign.

Mainnet: set Chain = mainnet when you create the pod, pair a wallet above, and fund it — that sends a real BCH mainnet transaction, signed by your wallet only.

What the connector guarantees

Server never sees a private keySigning happens on your phone / in your wallet, over the relay.
Verify before broadcastReturned bytes are decoded and checked: all signatures valid, pod output exactly the requested amount. Anything else = 400, no broadcast. Tested against a dishonest wallet in tests/WalletGuard.test.mjs (16/16).
Covenant is the authorityEven a perfectly signed wallet transaction cannot exceed the window, redirect the payee, or refund early — the VM refuses it.
