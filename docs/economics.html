Economics

Two numbers decide whether an agent-payment rail is real: what a settlement costs, and who pays for it. Both are measured on chain, not modelled.

1 · What settlement costs

Measured on chipnet by scripts/batch-run.mjs. The pod pays N chunks per input, so the fee is amortised across payments instead of repeated.

The covenant refuses to merge two pods into one transaction — that would wreck per-pod accounting. So settlement scales by paying many chunks per input rather than many pods per transaction. On chains with meaningful fees, the per-payment cost is what decides whether an agent can afford to pay per inference at all.

4 · Why the incumbent options lose on these numbers

OptionWhy it fails an agent paying per unit of work
Funded hot wallet (raw x402 style)No ceiling and no refunds: one buggy or hostile provider drains the budget, and a garbage response is final. Documented as a gap by the standard's own backers.
Custodial platform (Stripe-for-agents)Requires KYC and an account per side; per-transaction pricing can't settle a tenth of a cent; settlement is T+2, not a block.
EVM escrow contractWorks, but gas makes per-chunk settlement absurd — the fee exceeds the payment for small chunks, so you have to batch off-chain and reintroduce a custodian.
ForemanBounded pull + expiry + refund, all enforced by the covenant. Per-chunk settlement is affordable, and the refund path is cheap enough to actually use.

5 · Who the first customer is

Agent frameworks and marketplaces that already move money to providers: inference resellers, GPU-time brokers, data-labeling and scraping pipelines, and any multi-step agent that subcontracts. The wedge is narrow and real: "stop giving your agent a funded wallet". The pitch is one integration, one covenant, and a spend ceiling that survives a compromised agent.

6 · What the prize money would fund

A third-party review of the covenant, a fee-policy parameter so budgets aren't constants, the payload-hash commitment that puts quality disputes on chain, and 20 conversations with agent builders who pay providers today.
